Inside the teaching

One child. One afternoon. Two very different parents.

In this teaching, we follow one child to a birthday party — twice. The same afternoon. The same child. What changes between the two versions is not a technique or a strategy. It is the inner state of the parent. Their understanding of adab. Their own relationship to gratitude and haya before Allah.
